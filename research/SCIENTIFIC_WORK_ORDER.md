## Current technical candidate

Polynomial-size, deterministically checkable certificates for either complete
hit schedules or unreachability in invertible affine prime-field recurrences.
The new derivation handles repeated primary factors through a deterministic
cyclic-unipotent membership calculation. The reference producer still contains
potentially expensive integer/polynomial factorization and finite-field
logarithm searches. No blanket polynomial-time producer is claimed. A separate proved precision-fiber
composition reuses these certificates over prime powers and composite moduli,
including an explicitly defined point-guard loop consumer. It does not promote
composite arithmetic into the primary prime-field schema.

## Scientific closure requirements

1. Audit the strongest direct finite-field matrix-logarithm predecessor in full,
   alongside the closest exact-algebra certificate literature. Attribute known
   arguments and identify precisely any new theorem, proof-system capability,
   cost bound, or independently useful combination. Absence from a search result
   does not establish novelty.
2. State the useful consuming task independently of the solver. Compare its
   actual required output, confidence, preprocessing, certificate size, checking
   and later-use costs with source-aware classical alternatives. Do not compare
   only against orbit enumeration or inflate a horizon as performance evidence.
3. Give every theoretical claim an explicit proof and domain, including proof
   production versus proof checking, repeated factors, invalid inputs, negative
   conclusions, and resource exhaustion. Identify unformalized checker trust.
4. Validate all new implementations with independent exact controls, mutated
   false certificates, reproducible fixtures, and appropriate native methods.
   Preserve counterexamples and failures. Do not alter old evidence silently.
5. Maintain an evidence map for eventual claims about motivation, prior gaps,
   contributions, consequences, limitations, and generality. It is a research
   source dossier, not a prematurely persuasive abstract or conclusion.
6. Run the original root verifier, existing independent audit and all new
   regression checks on the integrated revision. A tested source subset must
   not be described as that final integration test.

Only mark scientific research complete when the ledger supports it. The current
ledger explicitly leaves novelty/significance and the complete consumer-level
comparison open. A correct prototype or an extensive test count cannot close
those two requirements by itself.

Keep the parent quantum project unchanged. Preserve the original license and
historical sources. No release, submission, external contact or paid computing
is part of this work order. Authorized ordinary development and integration
remain separate from manuscript preparation.

## Positive-certificate simplification

[Direct modular hit certificates](direct_modular_hits_v1/README.md) remove the unnecessary full precision chain for positive answers. The standard point-order witness works over any explicit residue ring with a checked inverse and needs no factorization of the modulus. A matched positive-proof comparison reduces the recorded 64-bit witness from 21,744 to 292 bytes. Native solving, proof assembly, and checking are separately costed. This repairs our implementation using established mathematics; it is not claimed to close the originality requirement. Complete negative evidence remains the role of the richer algebraic construction.
